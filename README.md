### Projects

**[Trading-system](https://github.com/harrywinter06-code/Trading-system)**: Autonomous daily trading pipeline. Ingests macro and market data from FRED, yfinance, Reddit and RSS feeds; runs an evolutionary strategy search with walk-forward validation; executes on Alpaca paper trading with circuit breakers. Separate research agents pull arXiv papers and market sentiment to feed the next generation of strategies.

**[clustertrace](https://github.com/harrywinter06-code/clustertrace)**: Local-first observability for LLM agents. Clusters agent traces by execution pattern to surface the failing clusters. SQLite-backed.

**[polymarket-edge](https://github.com/harrywinter06-code/polymarket-edge)**: Depth- and cost-aware no-arbitrage scanner for Polymarket negRisk events, plus a Hyperliquid funding-capture backtest.

**[gina-starter-pack](https://github.com/harrywinter06-code/gina-starter-pack)**: Polymarket negRisk basket-arbitrage strategy packaged for Ask Gina, with a three-layer pipeline and tests.

**internship-hunter** *(private)*: Automated internship application pipeline. Discovers roles from Remotive, Greenhouse/Lever ATS boards, Reed.co.uk and funding news feeds; generates tailored cover letters and cold emails using Claude with prompt caching; manages an email queue with Hunter.io lookup and Gmail delivery through a Streamlit dashboard.
